Welcome to **Jewel Brook: Heroes of Embryon**, an offline-first gemstone puzzle adventure and working-kingdom simulation with mining, farming, cooking, trading and a royal timeline. The project was previously named Rupee Rush; the authoritative source is [mrcalzon02/Jewel_Brook](https://github.com/mrcalzon02/Jewel_Brook).

## Canon and technical source of truth

The live **offline World Guide** is implemented in [`web/embryon-world-guide.js`](https://github.com/mrcalzon02/Jewel_Brook/blob/main/web/embryon-world-guide.js), with its longer written reference in [`docs/EMBRYON_WORLD_WIKI.md`](https://github.com/mrcalzon02/Jewel_Brook/blob/main/docs/EMBRYON_WORLD_WIKI.md). The campaign, Chronicle, trials, crops, recipes, equipment and economy have **separate code-owned definitions**. A Wiki page explains the implementation; it does not override those gameplay registries.

For the latest tested artifacts, use [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/mrcalzon02/Jewel_Brook/releases), **including prereleases**. For developer source and maintenance rules, see the [README](https://github.com/mrcalzon02/Jewel_Brook/blob/main/README.md) and [platform deployment status](https://github.com/mrcalzon02/Jewel_Brook/blob/main/docs/DEPLOYMENT_TARGETS.md).

The project is actively developed. No claim of full Windows/Linux parity, Google Play publication, completed art production, or tested installation on every Fire tablet is made here.
