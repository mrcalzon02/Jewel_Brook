# Daysha — Jewel Brook playable character contract

**Status:** Visual direction approved 2026-10-23. Source image: `crystal_princess_of_the_sky_palace.png`; SHA-256 `667672081d8c59d0c1aaded6b46f185d6f1728b6d15628832d58c3234e43cf30`. Source was approved directly in the project conversation.

## Face and identifying silhouette
Adult noble elf; pale warm complexion; long icy/silver-blue flowing hair with interwoven ornaments; violet irises; long pointed ears; confident, bright, approachable face. These persist at all expression levels, distances and outfits. Jewelry, brows, iris and ears are especially important in a 96px portrait.

## Approved Jewelbrook default outfit
Ivory sculpted bodice, royal-blue structured skirt, lilac semi-sheer cascading sleeves, fine gold leaf filigree, sapphire/blue crystal jewels and gold sandals. Her trademark prop is an original glowing **Embryon crystal shard**. This default costume was approved *in the new game style*, and supersedes the earlier default dark-scale costume suggested in provisional template documents.

## Alternate outfits
The user's established Noble Elf reference permits dark blue-black scale armor, silver pauldrons, blue cloak and leather belts for a campaign battle/expedition variation. Formal and moon-queen interpretations are other future variants, **not** automatic replacements for the approved key-art look.

## Production deliverables
1. Transparent full-body master: face/ears/hair, hands, ankles and glowing shard inside padded bounds.
2. Three-quarter campaign bust: neutral, curious, determined, delighted, surprised, victorious.
3. 96px, 128px and 256px square portrait views designed for legibility, not blindly resized full-body key art.
4. Separate layered accessory/prop art if Daysha will be animated in UI.
5. Expression identity audit: same face landmarks, proportion, color, parting and ear length.
6. Signed-off alpha/crop, PNG/WebP export, manifest, in-game screenshot and mobile QA.

## Hard rejection criteria
Blond/auburn hair, green irises, interchangeable generic elf face, an iconic green tunic, borrowed Zelda imagery, shortened/hidden ears, cut-off tips/shadows, unreadable under-44px face, or broad bloom obliterating features. A technically high-resolution render that fails these is **rejected**. Do not label approved until checked against the image named above.
