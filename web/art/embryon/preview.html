Production criteria

Actual sprite files need unique readable tile silhouettes, true-alpha backgrounds, intact safety gutters, colorblind legibility, 32px review, 44px tap targets, material consistency, manifest proofs and browser QA. Full specifications are in docs/ART_STYLE_MASTER.md.
